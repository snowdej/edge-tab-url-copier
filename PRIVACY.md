# Privacy Policy — Group & Selected Tab URL Copier

_Last updated: 2026-11-20_

This extension does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

- **No network access.** The extension makes no network requests and loads no remote code.
- **Tab URLs** are read only when you trigger a copy action, and only for the tabs being copied. They are written to your clipboard and nowhere else.
- **Pasted URLs** are used only to open the tabs you asked for.
- **Clipboard:** the extension writes to the clipboard when you ask it to copy. It never reads the clipboard.
- **Stored data:** the only thing saved is your line-ending preference (CRLF or LF). It's kept in browser extension storage, which Edge may sync between your own signed-in devices.
- **No analytics, no tracking, no third parties.**

Questions: open an issue on the project's repository.
